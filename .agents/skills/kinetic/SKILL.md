# Kinetic Design System Skill

## Mission
Build dense, technical interfaces that feel fast, clear, and operational — speed and clarity over softness.

## Brand
Kinetic design is a dense technical system with JetBrains Mono typography, a kinetic yellow brand, deep navy accents, flat uniform surfaces, and borderless lifted cards.

## Style Foundations
- Visual style: dense, technical, sharp, controlled
- Typography: JetBrains Mono as the primary UI voice; compact, operational hierarchy
- Color direction:
  - Kinetic yellow for primary actions and brand emphasis (e.g. `#F5C518`)
  - Deep navy for foreground accents and high-emphasis structure (e.g. `#0F1C3F`)
  - One uniform section surface; cards lift via a lighter derivative of that surface
  - Inputs read through contrasting fills, not borders
  - Separators are precise gradient hairlines, not heavy rules
- Geometry: 4px corners for box-shaped shells; fully pill toggles and badges only where semantics require it
- Depth: no drop shadows on resting surfaces; no bordered cards or inputs; brand focus ring for active states

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA. Keep yellow-on-navy and yellow-on-surface contrast valid. Visible focus rings are sharp and deliberate.

## Writing Tone
Terse, operational, precise.

## Rules: Do
- Keep every section on one uniform surface language
- Lift cards with a lighter surface derivative, not borders or shadows
- Use yellow for brand/action moments; navy for structure and emphasis
- Prefer compact density suitable for dashboards and ops tools

## Rules: Don't
- No softness, decorative clutter, or rounded marketing-card language
- No bordered cards/inputs as the default
- No drop shadows on resting surfaces
- No decorative status colors unless a real state needs them

## Best for
Engineering dashboards, trading/finance tools, logistics, infrastructure, command centers, data-heavy SaaS, and AI operations screens.
