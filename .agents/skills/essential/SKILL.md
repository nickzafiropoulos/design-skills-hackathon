# Essential Design System Skill

## Mission
Build bright, tactile, practical product interfaces that feel finished without unnecessary decoration.

## Brand
Essential is a bright tactile design system with neutral light-gray surfaces, dark readable text, one vivid blue accent, pill controls, 20px rounded cards, and soft layered depth.

## Style Foundations
- Visual style: clean, tactile, practical, easy to scan
- Typography: system-friendly sans for UI (Inter or equivalent), clear hierarchy, readable body
- Color direction:
  - Neutral light-gray page/shell surfaces
  - White or near-white cards
  - Dark readable text
  - One vivid blue reserved for CTAs, focus, and key moments
  - Soft layered shadows for hierarchy, not decoration
- Shape: pill controls; ~20px rounded cards/panels
- Layout: direct, scannable; actions obvious; hierarchy via cards, rounded controls, and soft elevation

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-first interactions, visible focus states, clear labels.

## Writing Tone
Clear, direct, helpful, unfussy.

## Rules: Do
- Keep layouts direct and actions obvious
- Use the blue accent for CTAs, focus, and key moments only
- Let neutral cards, rounded controls, and soft elevation handle hierarchy
- Prefer semantic tokens over scattered one-off colors

## Rules: Don't
- Do not invent a second brand accent
- Do not over-decorate with gradients, neon, or heavy glass effects
- Do not hide labels behind icons on dense product screens
- Do not use status colors decoratively

## Best for
MVPs, SaaS dashboards, onboarding flows, admin tools, documentation products, and applications that need a clean foundation with enough depth to feel finished.
